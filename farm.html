🛏️ Go to sleep?

Resting will restore your energy and advance to the next day. Crops will continue to grow overnight.

❓ How to Play

🕹️ Joystick (left) moves your character.

✦ Action button (right) uses your current tool on the tile in front of you.

🎒 Bag button opens tools & inventory. Tap a tool to equip it. Tap a seed to plant (you must hold the seed and stand on tilled, watered or dry soil).

Tools:
⛏️ Hoe – tills grass into soil
💧 Watering Can – waters soil (refill at the lake)
🌾 Sickle – harvests ripe crops & cuts grass
🪓 Axe – chops trees (gets wood)
⛏ Pickaxe – breaks rocks (gets stone)

Loop: Till → Plant a seed → Water daily → Wait days → Harvest → Sell at shop → Buy better seeds!

🏪 Walk up to the shop (bottom right of the map) to buy seeds and sell crops.

🛏️ Walk up to your bed in the house to sleep & advance the day. Energy fully refills.

🎯 Goal: Build the biggest farm and reach 5,000g!
